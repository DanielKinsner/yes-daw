1. Request magic is the eight ASCII bytes `YCSREQ02`; its u32 version is2. Terminal magic is `YCSRES02`; its u32 version is2. A v2 producer/reader admits only these v2 values; it does not coerce or accept v1. Other offsets/extents remain request140, compiler41,260, native18,644, terminal308 and full transport79,472 bytes.

SHA256 and all current-root/request/binary/fence checks remain required. The label change is not loaded-image proof.
3. Add reason value48, name `JobTerminationFailure`, allowed site10 (`JobSettlement`) only. All0..47 reasons keep their v1 meanings and allowed sites. JobQueryFailure32 remains an actual job-query failure. No other pair is added.

Record the reached cleanup group's reap-API-failure bit1; preserve its precedence over exit-unobserved bit2 and preserve first-primary/secondary/native-secondary ordering. Do not overwrite an earlier failure or invent an exit/empty-job observation. An actual later job-query failure is a distinct32 event. The existing status2/3/4, known/unknown, helper-exit and parent quarantine matrices still determine outcome from independently observed reached owners and proofs.

Header/terminal layout, owned names/counts, raw CLI, target stdin/environment/quoting, all flags, cleanup maximum15, parent keys, whole source/storage/log/stage/time policies, same-origin prefix/prepaid tail and all later review/execution gates remain unchanged. Source/manifest/checker/correspondence/census must adopt and pin this exact revision; equal extents grant no price, ABI, fit or execution credit.
